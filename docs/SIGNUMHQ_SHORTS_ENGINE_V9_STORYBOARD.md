# SIGNUMHQ SHORTS ENGINE — V9 MASTER STORYBOARD

## A. Why V8 Failed
MarketPressureBrief V8 attempted to extend the duration to 40 seconds, but doing so without a strong underlying narrative made it feel slow and empty.
1. **Low Insight Density:** The video showed data but lacked the "why should I care?" payoff.
2. **Visual Clutter & Emptiness:** Some frames had colliding text, while others felt sparse.
3. **Passive Backgrounds:** The generated atmospheric assets were muddy and operated as passive wallpaper rather than timed impact moments.
4. **Abstract Messaging:** Despite using concrete numbers ("1.3%"), the connection to *actual market pressure* was entirely missing. It remained a visual concept demo, not a premium intelligence brief.

## B. Final Core Message
**Primary Message:**
"SPY is 1.3% below a hidden Call Wall. A normal chart shows price. SignumHQ reveals where options pressure may concentrate."

**Viewer Takeaway:**
"SPY is near a hidden options wall that normal charts do not show, and pressure may concentrate there."

## I. Typography and Layout Rules
**Typography Scale:**
- **Hero Hook**: 72px, Weight 900, `Inter` or `Outfit`, Tracking 0.05em.
- **Insight Headline**: 64px, Weight 800, Tracking 0.02em.
- **Data Number (e.g., 1.3%)**: 96px, Weight 900, Amber/Cyan.
- **Supporting Label**: 34px, Weight 700, Tracking 0.1em.
- **Disclaimer**: 22px, Weight 500, opacity 0.5.

**Layout Zones (Strict):**
- **Top Zone (Y: 10% - 30%)**: Reserved for Insight Headlines.
- **Center Zone (Y: 30% - 70%)**: Reserved for dynamic data (Wall lines, Price line, Bracket, Toggle UI).
- **Bottom Zone (Y: 70% - 90%)**: Reserved for captions or Product Need text.
- **Safety Margins**: 100px padding on Left/Right. 200px padding on Top/Bottom (Mobile safe areas).

**Color Rules:**
- **White/Off-White**: Primary narrative text.
- **Cyan**: SPY Price, Product Layer.
- **Coral**: Call Wall, Threat/Pressure.
- **Amber**: Distance/Gap metrics (1.3%).
- **Purple**: Gamma Flip.
- **Emerald**: Put Floor.
