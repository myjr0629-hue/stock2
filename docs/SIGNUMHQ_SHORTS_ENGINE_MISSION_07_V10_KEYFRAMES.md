## 4. Visual Intent & Psychology per Frame
- **00 Hook**: "YOUR CHART IS MISSING A LAYER." Pure scroll-stopping power. Massive typography layered over a deeply atmospheric, almost invisible monolith. No data to distract.
- **01 Concrete Insight**: The hard data payload. "SPY IS 1.3% BELOW A HIDDEN CALL WALL". The bracket and metrics give an immediate sense of scale and precision.
- **02 Why Care**: "NEAR WALLS, PRESSURE MAY CLUSTER." Shifts from hard data to conceptual threat/opportunity. The visual pressure zone (cyan/coral) activates.
- **03 Pressure Cluster**: "THE GAP IS ONLY 1.3%". Extreme camera zoom. The frame physically tightens around the gap, escalating tension.
- **04 Pressure Map**: "NOT A PREDICTION. A PRESSURE MAP." Establishes authority. The full 3-tier structure cleanly scales out.
- **05 Product Difference**: Split view. The top half is the drab, useless "NORMAL CHART". The bottom half is the glowing, data-rich "SIGNUMHQ LAYER". Unavoidable product superiority.
- **06 CTA**: Brand memory. Clean finish.
