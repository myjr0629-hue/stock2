# SIGNUM HQ — App Store / Play Store Listing (US · JP · KR)

Ready-to-paste store copy + ASO strategy for launch. Character limits noted per field.
App = free, ad-supported market-intelligence app (AI sector reports, options/gamma/flow,
morning & closing briefs). **Compliance:** research/education/data only — never framed as
investment advice or buy/sell signals (Apple 3.1.x / Google finance policy + legal safety).

---

## 0. Core positioning (all markets)
- **One-liner:** Institutional-grade market intelligence, organized — AI sector reports, options gamma, and money-flow, free.
- **Pillars:** (1) AI sector intelligence & closing/morning briefs (2) Options structure — GEX, PCR, gamma regime, max pain (3) Money flow — dark pool, whale activity, net premium (4) Multi-market, 3 languages.
- **Do NOT use words:** "signals to buy", "guaranteed", "profit", "returns", "recommendation", "advice". **Use:** "intelligence", "analysis", "research", "insight", "data", "read".
- **Category:** Finance (secondary: News if allowed).
- **Age rating:** 17+ (frequent/intense financial info) — safest for a market app; confirm at submission.

## 4. ASO strategy & launch-buzz notes
- **Title keyword:** put the single highest-intent keyword in the app name after the brand (US "Stock Market Intel", KR "AI 시장 인텔리전스"). Brand first for recognition.
- **iOS keywords field:** never repeat words already in the name/subtitle (wasted characters); no spaces after commas; singular forms (Apple stems plurals).
- **First 3 screenshots + first 3 description lines** drive most installs — lead with the AI sector report + options gauge visuals, not a feature list.
- **Localize, don't translate** the screenshots' on-image captions per market (done above).
- **Ratings prompt:** trigger the in-app review request after a positive moment (e.g., 3rd session opening a report), never on launch.
- **Initial buzz:** submit all 3 locales together; seed with a "featured today" internal-test group; ask early users for reviews in the first 72h (ranking weight). Keep the promo text fresh (updatable without re-review).
- **A/B (later):** test subtitle variants ("options flow" vs "market intelligence") and screenshot order via App Store Connect / Play experiments.
- **Compliance safety:** every locale's description ends with the "not investment advice" line — keep it; it protects review + legal.
