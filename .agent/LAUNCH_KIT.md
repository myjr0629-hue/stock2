**본문**:
> Hi HN. I built SIGNUM HQ — a free iOS/Android app that surfaces the options-positioning and money-flow data institutions watch but retail rarely sees: dark-pool %, gamma exposure (GEX), max pain, call walls / put floors, net premium — computed for ~1,860 tickers daily, updated intraday.
>
> The itch: most retail tools show you a price and call it analysis. Desks don't trade on price — they trade on *positioning*: where dealers are short gamma, where the max-pain pin sits, where dark-pool prints stack. That layer is normally paywalled or buried. I wanted it on my phone, in plain language.
>
> How it works: a pipeline pulls options/flow/dark-pool inputs, computes the positioning metrics + a "divergence" signal (when the news narrative and the money flow disagree), caches to Redis, and serves a Capacitor remote-webview app. An LLM writes plain-language reads — compliance-scrubbed, it never says buy/sell. en/ko/ja.
>
> Free, ad-supported, no account. Not investment advice — a data/education lens.
>
> I'd love feedback on the **data itself** (accuracy, what's missing) and whether the "divergence" framing is useful signal or noise.
>
