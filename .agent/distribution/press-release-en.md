## Solo-Built Trio of Free Apps Puts Institutional-Grade US Stock Data in Anyone's Pocket

**— SIGNUM HQ visualizes options flow and dark pool activity; Undercurrent flags the gap
between news and money; Why'd It Move? teaches investing through daily quizzes —**

SIGNUM HQ LLC today announced the global availability of three free mobile apps on the
App Store and Google Play, localized in English, Korean, and Japanese. All three work
without an account.

**SIGNUM HQ: Stock Market Intel** shows retail investors the data institutional traders
watch — options flow, dark pool share, gamma exposure (GEX), and max pain — for free.
After each close, AI-generated reports cover ten sectors.

**Undercurrent: News & Money** reads the same data for a general audience. Its signature
feature flags the "divergence" when news sentiment and actual institutional money point
in opposite directions. It publishes two to three editions daily.

**Why'd It Move?: Stock Quiz** teaches "why did this stock move today" through daily
multiple-choice questions, each answer backed by the ticker's real institutional data.

All three apps were built by a single developer using AI coding tools. "The information
gap between institutions and individuals is an access problem," the company said. "Our
goal is to move data that lived behind expensive terminals into free apps."

All data is for informational and educational purposes only and is not investment advice
or a recommendation to buy or sell any security.
