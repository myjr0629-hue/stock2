### en
Every night, today's US market writes the quiz.
"Why did this stock move today?" Investigate real charts and real data from stocks that actually moved — in three minutes.

WHAT IT IS
- Today's real movers become today's questions — live data, never canned examples
- A 4-choice cause investigation: institutional flow, sector rotation, peer moves, macro
- After the reveal: the receipt article plus an institutional deep layer (dark pool, options structure)
- Six interactive plays: Level Hunt, Number Sense (focus drills), Replay, Macro Domino, News Lesson, Weekend Review
- A 4-track curriculum: chart reading → institutional footprints → macro currents → reading the news
- A living glossary of 36 terms — each opens with today's live value
- Gut Accuracy: your rolling 30-day judgment curve
- Streaks, XP, belts — game-like, but calm

FOR
- Beginners who find market news intimidating
- Intermediates curious what institutions actually did behind the chart
- Anyone who wants a 3-minute daily habit

Free. No account. Educational information only — not investment advice.

## 5. 심사 노트 (Review Notes, en — 양 스토어 공용)
Why'd It Move? is a daily market-education quiz app by SIGNUM HQ LLC (our third app; SIGNUM HQ and Undercurrent are live under the same architecture).
- Content loads from our own domain (signumhq.com) inside a Capacitor shell with native features: splash, status bar, haptics, in-app review prompt, Android hardware-back handling, device-locale routing (en/ko/ja).
- No account, no login — everything works immediately on first launch. Learning progress is stored on-device only.
- No ads in this version. No purchases.
- Education only: the app explains market concepts with real historical/current data. It never gives buy/sell recommendations; a disclaimer ("not investment advice") is shown in-app.
- To test: launch → (optional onboarding) → tap the big "investigate" button on the hero card → answer the 4-choice quiz → see explanation + data layer. The four bottom tabs (Home/Dictionary/Search/Records) are all reachable without any setup.

## 7. 남은 절차 (제출 직전 체크)
1. ⏳ iOS 시뮬 빌드 검증(진행 중) → 실기기 스모크(대표님 아이폰, Xcode 서명: Team=Signum Hq LLC 선택만)
2. Android: `cd wim-app/android && JDK21로 ./gradlew bundleRelease` — **키스토어 결정 필요**: UC 업로드키 재사용 가능(동일 키로 다중 앱 서명 허용) or 신규 생성. keystore.properties는 gitignored 유지
3. ASC: 새 앱 생성(com.signumhq.wim) → 위 자료 입력 → 빌드 업로드(Xcode Organizer) → 심사 제출
4. Play Console: 새 앱 → 자료 입력 → AAB 업로드 → 국가(US/JP/KR+) → 제출
5. 제출 = 대표님 계정 액션. 빌드·업로드까지는 준비 완료 상태로 인계
